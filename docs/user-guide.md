# Orbit user guide

Orbit runs its models on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone unless you choose the Gemini cloud model, and Orbit asks before sending to it.

## Models
Settings > Models shows models that suit your phone first. Downloads run in the background and resume if interrupted. To use a file you already have, tap **Import model file** and choose a `.litertlm` or `.task` file; set its name, prompt style, context size and whether it understands images. Orbit test-loads it once and tells you if it can't use it.

## Chat
- Start a chat from Home. Hold a message for copy, regenerate and other actions; the speaker icon reads a reply aloud (speed and voice: Settings > Voice).
- Attach photos (take a photo or pick one) to models that understand images.
- Search all chats with the search button; pin, rename, archive and delete from the chat list; export a chat as PDF, Markdown or text from the chat menu.

## Spaces
A Space is a set of documents Orbit can answer from. Add PDF, Word (.docx), EPUB or text files. Indexing runs in the background and shows progress. Answers list the documents and pages used; tap a source to see the excerpt.

## Memory
Orbit offers "Remember this?" when you share a lasting fact. Tap to save it. Manage entries in Settings > Memory; turn Memory off there to stop using it.

## Tools
Ask in your own words, for example "send hi to Aditya on WhatsApp", "remind me every Friday at 6 pm to submit expenses", "set a timer for 10 minutes", "navigate to the airport", or "turn on the flashlight". Orbit shows what it will do and waits for your confirmation; messages and calls need your final tap in the other app. Switch any tool off in Settings > Tools.


## Orbit Bubble
Enable it in Settings > Orbit Bubble (needs "draw over other apps" and the microphone). Tap the bubble and speak. Tap **Reply** on the answer card to ask a follow-up; **Continue in app** saves the exchange as a chat. Turn on "Save bubble conversations to history" to keep every bubble chat.

## Text selection and sharing
Select text in any app and choose **Orbit** to proofread, rewrite, translate, summarise, explain or draft a reply; **Replace** puts the result back where the text was. Translate uses Hindi unless you pick another language ("Translate to another language…"). Share files or images to Orbit from any app to use them in a chat or add them to a Space.

## Modes
A mode is a reusable assistant: instructions, optional model and Spaces, and a colour. Start from a template, or import and export modes as files.

## Backup and privacy
- Settings > Backup & restore makes one passphrase-protected file with chats, memories, modes and settings. API keys are left out unless you include them. Downloaded models aren't included. Restoring replaces the data on the phone and restarts Orbit.
- Settings > Privacy center shows what is stored, a 30-day list of the app's network calls (host and reason only), the permissions in use, and **Delete everything**.

## Reminders
Settings > Tools > Saved reminders lists reminders (daily, weekly, weekday, monthly and every-N repeats). Notifications offer Snooze (10 minutes or 1 hour) and Done.
